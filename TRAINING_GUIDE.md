# Training in ChessAsh

Training turns mistakes in your reviewed games into positions you can practice. It uses your side of the game and keeps practice results separate from the saved review.

## Get your positions ready

1. Import games from your Chess.com account or open a PGN.
2. Set your main account in Library. For a PGN, choose your colour under Review as.
3. Complete a game review, then open Training in the top navigation.

Sample games, your opponent's moves and games reviewed from a neutral perspective do not become personal exercises. If Training has no positions, check your selected side and finish a review. Very shallow reviews may need deeper analysis before they provide exercises.

## Choose a session

Start training opens a mixed session of up to five positions due for practice. It starts with a high-priority position and varies the problems and source games that follow.

Practice this opens a session for one group. Depending on your reviewed games, groups can include undefended pieces, forks, back-rank mates, captures, checks and opponent replies. The app uses specific groups when the saved analysis supports them.

## Try a move

The board faces your side. Choose a legal move to improve on what happened in the game. Sound alternatives can be accepted even when they differ from the saved best move.

If your move gives away an opportunity, the feedback explains a concrete opponent reply when the engine supplies one. Select Show opponent's reply to animate it. Continue the line follows more replies; Back to your move restores the position after your attempt.

Try again restores the exercise. Show solution plays the saved best move. Original game opens the source position's context, with more explanation after you solve or reveal it.

Open source position takes you directly to that move in the original game review. Return to Training to continue the exercise.

## Pick up where you left off

An unfinished session is saved automatically, including the current exercise, recorded answer and reply-preview position. If you close ChessAsh while Training is open, it reopens there. An interrupted analysis restarts from the exercise so an unfinished check does not count as an attempt.

Sessions belong to the active practice profile. Changing accounts uses a separate session. If a source game is removed or reanalysis replaces its exercise, an outdated session is not restored.

## Review your session

The recap shows positions solved on the first try, those solved after another attempt and those where you revealed the answer. It lists what to revisit and when the next practice is due. Practice these again lets you immediately revisit positions that needed work.

Solving a position after revealing its answer remains assisted. It does not count as an independent success. Successful independent attempts come back at longer intervals; missed and revealed positions return sooner.

## Progress and settings

Practice history is saved locally for the selected main account. PGNs with an explicitly selected side use a separate practice profile when no main account is configured. Changing the main account changes the active practice history.

The interface and coaching support English and Italian. Reduced motion also applies to training. Standard training runs locally and needs no AI model or API key.

Progress over time shows independent solves by pattern across completed sessions. It compares recent positions with earlier ones and shows a trend after two complete groups of five positions across at least two sessions. Revealed answers do not count as independent solves. With fewer results, you see the recorded counts while more sessions accumulate.
