# Personalized training

The Training dashboard shows your next session, practice by pattern, and first-try results. The practice screen tracks attempts, and the recap shows what to revisit. The native UI is in `native/src/training_ui.rs`, and both platforms use the bundled Foxash training rules. See the [Training guide](../TRAINING_GUIDE.md) for how to practice.
