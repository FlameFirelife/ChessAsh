# ChessAsh v1.1

For this update, I focused on making reviews easier to follow and the board more responsive.

## What changed

- I made the explanations more specific to each position, so they repeat themselves less and tell you which pieces and squares are involved.
- You can hover over or tap a square name in an explanation to highlight it on the board.
- The best move, arrow and highlights now appear immediately. I removed the Hint button from the review, since you're there to see the explanation.
- You can switch between the position before your move and what happened after it. The board shows the move, attacked pieces and relevant replies with arrows and highlights.
- Pieces animate when you move them or switch positions. You can still turn animations down with reduced motion.
- **Try again** is still there when you want to practise a position yourself.

Your saved reviews get the new explanations too. I haven't changed review speed in this update; that's something I'll work on later.
