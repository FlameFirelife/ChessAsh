# ChessAsh v1.2

I focused on making the review easier to read and keeping the board steady while you use it.

- Added Italian for the interface and explanations.
- Explanations now speak from your side of the game. You can choose your side when importing a PGN too.
- Short, direct comments come first. You can expand the full explanation inside the same message.
- The board stays in place when comments change or you open more detail.
- Removed the Before/After buttons. The played position and best-move guidance share one view, with simpler arrows that don’t overlap.
- The app stays on Review while you try moves or follow a continuation. Explore opens only when you select it.
- Tried moves get their own feedback and classification without changing the saved game review.
- Smoothed out piece movement, captures and move feedback, and reduced flashing during updates.

Analysis speed is unchanged in this update.
