# ChessAsh v1.3

I added personalized training based on your own games.

- Training finds recurring problems in your reviewed moves and turns missed opportunities into positions you can practice.
- Your results shape what comes up next. Positions you struggle with return sooner, and solved positions return later for another try.
- You can practice a specific problem or start a short mixed session. Sound alternative moves are checked too.
- Failed attempts explain the opponent’s reply, which you can play through on the board.
- The session recap shows what you solved, where you needed help and what to revisit.
- Unfinished training sessions resume when you return, and exercises can open their original review position.
- Progress by pattern shows how your results change across completed sessions.
- Mixed sessions include more variety, with specific practice for undefended pieces, forks and back-rank mates.
- The review has less empty space above the board, and the small helper text is gone.
- Animations got reworked.
