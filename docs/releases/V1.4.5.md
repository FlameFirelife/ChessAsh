# ChessAsh v1.4.5

I improved ChessAsh’s explanations and made mobile reviews more compact.

- Explanations connect mistakes to defenders leaving, opened lines, forks, pins and mating threats when the analysed line supports them.
- Tactics that appear later in a line explain the earlier change that made them possible.
- Hints point to the piece or line involved in the mistake.
- Suggested moves explain why they help. The blue summary stays short, with the fuller reasoning under Full explanation.
- Review, Training replies and the tutor share the same position-based reasoning.
- More varied wording keeps explanations from repeating the same phrases.
- Mobile review has a compact top bar and tighter game details, giving more room to the explanation, board and move controls.
