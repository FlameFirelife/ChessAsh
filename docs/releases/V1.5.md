# ChessAsh v1.5

I added Lichess imports and shareable review cards, and improved sacrifice detection.

- Import finished Lichess games by username or game link. Lichess accounts have their own collections and can stay synced as your main account.
- Sound sacrifices are easier to recognise, including declined offers and sacrifices that save a draw by stalemate.
- Reanalyse now starts a fresh engine review. Continue review resumes a paused review.
- Settings can use Chess.com move-quality names, including Brilliant and Great.
- Move-quality icons match across the review, move list and board. Opening/Book uses the book icon.
- Move sounds have a softer wooden tock, with smoother playback when skipping quickly through moves.
- Share review creates PNG cards for best moves, biggest mistakes and better alternatives. Player names are optional, and every card includes the ChessAsh repository link.
- Android can share a card directly through the phone’s sharing menu, or save the PNG separately.
