# ChessAsh v1.6

I rebuilt ChessAsh in Rust for Windows and Android. The board still has the original pieces, the wooden move sound, and the spotlight for sound sacrifices.

- Stockfish 19 Lite runs directly on both platforms. Supported CPUs use AVX2 on Windows or Arm dot-product instructions on Android. Foxash still handles review and training rules.
- The coach explains why a move works or what it gives away. Choose your side under **Review as** for explanations from your perspective. Tap a linked square to see it on the board.
- The blue best-move note stays short. **Full explanation** adds the reasoning, and **Show line** plays the continuation.
- The move controls line up under the board, with more room between the single and double arrows. The mobile layout gives the board and coach more room, and Windows opens maximized.
- Training puts your next session up front, with practice by pattern and first-try results across sessions.

Install V1.6 over ChessAsh to keep your library, saved reviews and settings. The Android APK is signed for in-place updates. The Windows installer is unsigned.
