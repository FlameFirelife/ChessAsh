# ChessAsh v1.7

I added a way to play a game in ChessAsh and reworked Training.

- Open **Play**, enter names for White and Black, and take turns on the same board. ChessAsh reviews moves as you play and explains why they work or what they give away.
- **Save to Library** puts the game under **PGN files and samples**. You can save partway through and save again to update the same game.
- I redid the Training dashboard, practice screen and recap. You can see your next session, choose a pattern to practice, and check which positions you solved on your first try.
- I fixed a slowdown when replaying the Sound sacrifice animation.

Install V1.7 over ChessAsh to keep your library, saved reviews and settings. The Android APK is signed for in-place updates. The Windows installer is unsigned.
